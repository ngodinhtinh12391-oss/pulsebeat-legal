Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026 · App: PulseBeat – Haptic Metronome (iOS)

Short version: PulseBeat does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data.

Data we collect

None. PulseBeat has no account system, no servers, no analytics, no crash-reporting SDKs and no advertising. It makes no network requests.

Data stored on your device

Only your settings (tempo, time signature, haptics, sound and appearance preferences) are saved on your iPhone so the app opens the way you left it. Deleting the app removes them.

Permissions

PulseBeat asks for no permissions: no microphone, camera, location, contacts, photos or tracking. Tap tempo uses touches, not sound.

Children

PulseBeat does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
