Need help or have an idea? Email kendyngo123@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

I don't feel any haptics

Check that Settings › Haptics is on inside PulseBeat, and that System Haptics is on in iOS Settings › Sounds & Haptics. Haptics need an iPhone with the Taptic Engine; without it PulseBeat uses sound and the visual pulse.

No click sound

Turn on Click sound in PulseBeat settings and raise the media volume. The click mixes with other music apps so you can play along.

Playback stops when I leave the app

In version 1.0 the metronome plays while PulseBeat is open. Use Pocket mode to keep the screen dark while you practise.

How do I stop Pocket mode?

Double-tap anywhere on the screen. A single tap wakes the display.
