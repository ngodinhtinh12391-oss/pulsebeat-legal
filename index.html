PulseBeat – Haptic Metronome

iPhone · Free · Works offline

PulseBeat taps every beat through the Taptic Engine, so you can keep time with your phone in a pocket or on your knee. Beat 1 taps strong, the rest tap light, with an optional click sound.

  • 40–240 BPM, slider and ±1 / ±5 nudges
  • Time signatures 2/4, 3/4, 4/4, 5/4, 6/8, 7/8 with accent
  • Tap tempo
  • Pocket mode: dark screen, double-tap to stop
  • English and Vietnamese, Light and Dark
